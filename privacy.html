1. Consent

By using our website (kalyanielectrical.in), submitting quotation or rate card inquiries, or accessing the Client Portal, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

2. Information We Collect

The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to provide your personal information:

  • Direct Inquiries & Quotations: When you contact us directly via WhatsApp, phone, or our website forms (Contact Us / Rate Card), we may receive your name, company/society name, phone number, site address in Pune or Maharashtra, and project requirements.
  • Client Portal Access: When you log into the Client Portal using your 10-digit registered mobile number, we use that identifier solely to retrieve and display your project quotations, invoices, and technical completion documents.
  • Partner Accounts Area: The Accounts section is password-protected for internal partner bookkeeping and stores data locally in your browser unless explicitly exported by an authorized partner.

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our electrical, LT panel, solar power, Low-TDS panel cleaning, CCTV, fire alarm, biometric access, VDP, and LAN networking services.
  • Prepare accurate BOQs, quotations, rate sheets, and tax invoices.
  • Schedule site surveys, daily visits, or on-site office execution in Pune and across Maharashtra.
  • Communicate with you directly through our dedicated partner desks (Amar, Munal, and Vishal) for customer service and maintenance support.
  • Prevent fraud and maintain the security of our digital portals.
